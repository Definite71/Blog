Buying Guide

How to Choose the Right Pair

Before you hit "Add to Cart", ask yourself these four questions.

What will you use it for?

Music = prioritise driver size and codec. Calls = look for ENC/ANC mics. Gaming = low latency mode is a must. Gym = IPX5+ water resistance.

What's your real budget?

Be honest — ₹1,000 buys surprising quality in 2026. Don't overspend for features you won't use daily. The ₹2,000–₹5,000 zone offers the best value per rupee.

iPhone or Android?

iPhone users: AirPods Pro 2 for seamless Spatial Audio. Android users: Sony WF-1000XM5 for LDAC lossless, or Galaxy Buds3 Pro for Samsung phones.

Do you travel often?

Frequent flyers need strong over-ear ANC — Sony WH-1000XM6 is worth every paisa. Casual commuters can get by with OnePlus Nord Buds 4 Pro at a fraction of the cost.

How long do you listen?

For 6+ hour daily sessions, prioritise total battery (case + buds). GOBOULT Z40 and boAt Rockerz 255 Pro+ offer 60 hours — outlasting most smartphones.

Wired or Wireless?

Wired = zero latency, no charging. Wireless = convenience and freedom. For gaming PCs, consider wired. For mobile and daily use, wireless is king.
